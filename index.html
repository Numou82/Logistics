About IK Developers Group

IK Developers Group is a construction and real-estate development company focused on industrial and warehousing projects around Mumbai. We plan, engineer, and build on our own land, carrying a project from site planning through structural steel erection to final handover.

Our focus is pre-engineered industrial construction: fast to build, built to last, and suited to how warehousing and logistics businesses actually operate. We work closely with our fabrication and material partners at every stage, so what's designed on paper is exactly what gets built on site.

How we build

1

Land & planning

We assess the site, plan the colony layout, and secure the approvals needed before any steel goes up.

2

Structural design

Structural steel frames are engineered for wide-span warehouse units, sized to the colony's layout.

3

Steel & deck fabrication

Structural steel and metal roof deck are fabricated with our partners to the project's specifications.

4

Erection & handover

Frame, roof, and wall cladding go up on site, through to final handover of each unit.

Featured project

IK Industrial Park, Mumbai

On our own land in Mumbai, we're developing a dedicated colony of warehouse units built as pre-engineered steel structures. Every unit is enclosed on the roof and all four walls in metal roof deck panelling — a system chosen for its speed of construction and long-term durability across a large industrial site.

Built in structural steel and metal deck

Metal roof deck is the backbone of every unit in the colony, used not only on the roof but across all four walls. Here's how the system comes together.

Roof — metal deck
Walls — metal deck cladding, all 4 sides
Frame — structural steel
Foundation — concrete footing

Fast to erect

Prefabricated steel sections and metal deck panels go up in weeks, shortening the time between foundation and handover.

Weather-tight

Profiled metal deck sheds Mumbai's monsoon rain quickly, with sealed roof-to-wall junctions on all four elevations.

Wide, column-free spans

Structural steel portal frames clear-span the width of each warehouse floor, keeping the working area open.

Low maintenance

Coated steel and deck panels are built to stand up to years of industrial use with minimal upkeep.
